Australian Terroir × Asian Heritage

Cubo takes its name from the kubo, the simple Filipino hut where people gather, close to nature. We cook with what the Australian land offers each season, rooted in the flavours, techniques and traditions of Asia. Not a fusion, but a meeting of cultures.

Meet Michelle

Chef & Owner

Michelle is the chef behind Cubo, guided by a simple belief: that food brings us closer to nature, culture and one another. With nearly 15 years in kitchens from five-star hotels to hatted restaurants, she cooks where Australian produce and Asian heritage meet, led by the season and shaped by fire.
