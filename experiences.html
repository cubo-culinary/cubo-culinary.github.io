Wilderness Feasts

Feasts shaped by nature, season and the open air.

Seasonal Australian produce and cooking rooted in Asian heritage, made over fire and shared at a long table in a natural setting. Each feast is designed around its surroundings: the landscape, the season and what grows nearby.

Open feast

Cubo sets the date, the place and the menu, and you book a seat. Come alone or with a friend, and share a set menu with fellow guests who arrive as strangers and leave as friends. Dates are announced on Instagram.
