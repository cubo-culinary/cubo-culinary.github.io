Wilderness Feasts

Feasts shaped by nature, season and the open air.

Rooted in the elements, this experience explores traditional ways of cooking and connecting with the land. Through open-fire cooking, ember roasting, preservation and native ingredients, each feast goes beyond conventional dining to celebrate the relationship between food, nature and traditional knowledge.
