Outdoors

Wilderness Feast Experience

Outdoor gatherings shaped by nature, season and the spirit of the open air.

A relaxed, immersive dining experience that brings Cubo into the outdoors — combining seasonal Australian produce, Asian-inspired cooking and communal dining in natural settings. From campfires and long tables to rustic cooking and shared plates, each feast is designed around its surroundings and the people gathered there.
