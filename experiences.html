Outdoors

Wilderness Feasts

Outdoor gatherings shaped by nature, season and the spirit of the open air.

Cubo in the outdoors: seasonal Australian produce and cooking rooted in Asian heritage, shared at communal tables in natural settings, for 20–30 guests. From campfires to long tables and shared plates, each feast is designed around its surroundings.

Bush Camp Classics

Comforting campfire favourites inspired by nostalgic outdoor cooking.

Ancestral Heritage

A celebration of Asian cultural traditions, heirloom recipes, and regional fire cooking passed down through generations.

Origins by Fire

An immersive exploration of humanity’s earliest relationship with fire: ancient techniques, Indigenous knowledge, foraging, fermentation, and seasonal ingredients.

Around the Fire Gatherings

A communal dining series where strangers become friends through the simple act of sharing food around the fire.
