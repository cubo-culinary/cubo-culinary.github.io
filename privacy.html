Privacy

How Cubo handles the details you share with us.

Last updated 4 November 2026

Who we are

Cubo is a private dining and events business based in Melbourne, Victoria. If you have a question about this policy or your information, email us at cuboculinary@gmail.com.

What we collect

When you send an enquiry through this website, we receive your name and email address, and anything else you choose to add: phone number, occasion, date, location, number of guests, preferred experience and dietary requirements. If you email us directly, we receive your message and email address.

We do not take payments or ask for payment details on this website.

How we use it

We use your details only to reply to your enquiry and to plan your dinner or event. Dietary requirements, including allergies, are used only to prepare your menu safely. We do not sell your information or use it for marketing without asking you first.

Services that help run this website

  • Web3Forms delivers enquiries from the form to our email inbox.
  • Google (Gmail) hosts our email, where enquiries and messages are kept.
  • Umami counts visits to this website: pages viewed, the site you came from, device type and country. It does not use cookies or identify you.
  • GitHub Pages hosts this website and may log IP addresses for security.

These services may store data outside Australia. Links to Instagram take you to Instagram, where Meta’s privacy policy applies.

Cookies

This website does not use cookies.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiries and emails for as long as we need them to respond, plan your event and keep reasonable business records. You can ask us to delete your information at any time.

Your choices

You can ask to see, correct or delete the information we hold about you by emailing cuboculinary@gmail.com. If you are not satisfied with our response, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner.

Changes

If we change how we handle your information, we will update this page and the date at the top.
