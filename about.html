About Cubo

What is Cubo

Cubo is a cross-cultural culinary experience exploring the intersection of Australian terroir × Asian heritage.

A simple space that brings people together.

Inspired by the spirit of the kubo — a simple space that brings people together, close to nature and the rhythms of everyday life — Cubo is built around the idea that food is never simply about what is on the plate.

We bring together Australian produce, seasonal landscapes and local producers with the traditions, techniques, flavours and stories of Asia. Through private dining, bespoke gatherings, supper clubs and immersive experiences, each menu responds to its surroundings, the season and the people gathered around the table.

Cubo is not about recreating tradition or simply combining cuisines. It is about respectful exchange, curiosity and evolution — finding new expressions of food while honouring where they come from.

At its heart, Cubo is about nature, culture, food and connection: creating experiences that encourage people to slow down, discover something new and share a meaningful meal together.

Philosophy

What we believe

i

Australian Terroir

We begin with the natural character of Australia — its landscapes, seasons, producers and produce. Nature sets the rhythm, allowing the land and what it offers to guide what we cook.

ii

Asian Heritage

We draw from the diverse culinary traditions of Asia — its flavours, techniques, ingredients and ways of gathering. These traditions are a source of knowledge and inspiration, approached with curiosity and respect.

iii

Cross-Cultural

Cubo is a meeting of cultures rather than a simple fusion of cuisines. Australian produce and Asian heritage come together to create food that feels connected to both, yet distinctly its own.

iv

Seasonal & Resourceful

We follow the rhythms of nature, cooking with ingredients when they are abundant and at their best. Through preservation, fermentation and thoughtful use, we extend their life, minimise waste and discover new expressions of flavour.

v

Curiosity & Connection

We believe food can bring us closer to nature, culture and one another. Every Cubo experience is an opportunity to discover, share and connect through the table.

Michelle Agustin · Chef | Founder

Meet Michelle

Cooking at the meeting point of two worlds.

Michelle founded Cubo around a simple belief: that food brings us closer to nature, culture and one another. Her cooking sits where Australian produce and Asian heritage meet — led by the season, shaped by fire, and always in conversation with the people at the table.

Through private dining, wilderness feasts and consulting, she is building a practice rooted in respect for origin, resourcefulness in the kitchen and hospitality that feels effortless.
