About the founder

Meet Michelle

Chef, creator and founder of Cubo.

Michelle Agustin · Chef | Founder

Cooking at the meeting point of two worlds

Michelle founded Cubo around a simple belief: that food brings us closer to nature, culture and one another. Her cooking sits where Australian produce and Asian heritage meet — led by the season, shaped by fire, and always in conversation with the people at the table.

Through private dining, wilderness feasts and consulting, she is building a practice rooted in respect for origin, resourcefulness in the kitchen and hospitality that feels effortless.
